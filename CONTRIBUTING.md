# Contributing to Hacker's Cheatsheet

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Hacker's Cheatsheet**!

## Scope & Ethical Boundaries

1. **Defensive & Authorized Testing Only**: Commands must be aimed at authorized penetration testing, vulnerability research, and systems administration. We do NOT accept destructive or malicious zero-day exploits.
2. **Portability & Client-Side Invariance**: The project must remain a lightweight, single-page client-side application. No remote tracking, telemetry, or backend servers.
3. **Accuracy & Clarity**: Ensure flags, syntax, and examples are tested against modern tooling (e.g., nmap, ffuf, radare2, gdb-pwndbg, metasploit).

## Workflow

1. Fork the repo and create your branch: `git checkout -b feat/new-commands`.
2. Add your section to `index.html` within the proper `<div class="section-container">`.
3. Submit a Pull Request with a clear description of the commands added.

Please adhere to our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
